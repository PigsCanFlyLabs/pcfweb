RETURNS

We have a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return. Electronics are an exception and are covered by the "Electronics" section below.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You'll also need the receipt or proof of purchase.

To start a return, you can contact us at support@pigscanfly.ca.

If your return is accepted, we'll send you instructions on how and where to send your package, and, for a return shipped from a United States address, a prepaid return shipping label. Items sent back to us without first requesting a return will not be accepted. Please note that if your country of residence is not United States, shipping your goods may take longer than expected.

You can always contact us for any return questions at support@pigscanfly.ca.

Restocking Fee

Accepted returns are subject to a 15% restocking fee, calculated on the purchase price of the returned item and deducted from your refund.

We do not charge a restocking fee when the return is our fault: an item that arrived defective, damaged, or was not the item you ordered, and electronics that arrive dead on arrival (see below). Nor do we charge it where the law does not permit us to.

Return Shipping

Returns shipped from a United States address: we email you a prepaid return shipping label with your return instructions. Use that label rather than a carrier of your own, so that we can track the package and so that you are not out of pocket.

Returns shipped from anywhere else: you arrange and pay for the shipping yourself, including any duties, taxes, or customs charges on the way back to us. We cannot send a prepaid label outside the United States. Please use a trackable service: we cannot refund a return that does not reach us.

Original shipping charges are not refunded, except where the law requires us to refund them — for an order shipped into the European Union, see the European Union section below.

The exception, again, is when the return is our fault: for an item that arrived defective, damaged, or wrong, and for electronics that arrive dead on arrival, we cover return shipping wherever you are. Inside the United States that is the prepaid label above; outside it, contact us before you ship, and we will reimburse what the return costs you.

Electronics

Electronics are not returnable. Once an electronic device has shipped, we cannot accept it back for a refund or an exchange, whether or not it has been opened or used, except when it arrives dead on arrival.

"Dead on arrival" means the device does not power on or does not function as described when you first receive it. Please inspect and test your device as soon as it arrives and contact us at support@pigscanfly.ca within 30 days of delivery if it is dead on arrival. We will ask you to describe the fault, and we may ask for a photo or a short video of it. If we confirm the device is dead on arrival, we will replace it or refund it in full, we will cover return shipping wherever you are (see "Return Shipping" above), and no restocking fee applies.

A device that develops a fault after it has been working is not dead on arrival; contact us anyway, because it may still be covered by the manufacturer's warranty and we will help you with a return-to-manufacturer-authorization (RMA).

This section does not affect any legal warranty or remedy you have under the consumer law of your country, state, or province, which applies whatever this policy says.

Damages and Issues

Please inspect your order upon receipt and contact us immediately if the item is defective, damaged, or if you receive the wrong item, so that we may evaluate the issue and make it right. Certain types of items cannot be returned, like perishable goods (such as food, flowers, or plants), custom products (such as special orders or personalized items), and personal care goods (such as beauty products). We also do not accept returns for hazardous materials, flammable liquids, or gases. Please get in touch if you have questions or concerns about your specific item.

Unfortunately, we cannot accept returns on sale items or gift cards, or activated/used devices.

Exchanges

The fastest way to ensure you get what you want is to return the item you have, and once the return is accepted, make a separate purchase for the new item. Note that the restocking fee and the return shipping cost above apply to the item you send back, and that electronics cannot be exchanged unless they arrive dead on arrival.

European Union 3 day cooling off period

Notwithstanding the above, if merchandise is being shipped into the European Union, you have the right to cancel or return your order within 3 days for any reason and without justification. As above, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging. You'll also need the receipt or proof of purchase. Where the law gives you this right, we will not apply the restocking fee, and the electronics exclusion above does not limit it. When you withdraw under this right, we also refund what you paid for the original delivery, up to the cost of our least expensive standard delivery: if you chose a faster service, the difference above the standard rate is not refunded. As the European Union is outside the United States, the return shipping itself is yours to arrange and pay for, unless the return is our fault.

Refunds

We will notify you once we've received and inspected your return to let you know if the refund was approved or not. If approved, you'll be automatically refunded on your original payment method, less the 15% restocking fee where it applies, within 10 business days. Please remember it can take some time for your bank or credit card company to process and post the refund too.

If more than 15 business days have passed since we've approved your return, please contact us at support@pigscanfly.ca.
