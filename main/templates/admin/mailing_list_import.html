Import addresses

A CSV: a Mailchimp or Google Forms export as it comes, or a bare column of addresses. Any heading with "email" in it is the address column and any with "name" in it are the name; everything else (Mailchimp's ratings, a Google Forms timestamp) is ignored, and a row that makes no sense is skipped rather than failing the upload. Addresses already on the chosen list are left alone, including anyone who unsubscribed from it.

Subscriber imports skip the confirmation email — an import is you saying you already have consent for these addresses. That is why the notice is worth leaving ticked: it tells people where they have ended up and how to leave.

Be careful importing onto All: that list receives every mailing addressed to any public list, so putting somebody there without them asking is the largest version of this mistake.

Every import is checked against the suppression list ({{ suppressed_count }} address{{ suppressed_count|pluralize:"es" }}), and nothing on it is ever added. Upload Mailchimp's unsubscribed and cleaned exports in suppress mode to fill it.
