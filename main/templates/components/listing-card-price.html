{% comment %} The price line and format summary on a product card, shared by every listing surface: /products (products.html) and the homepage carousels (index.html). One partial rather than a copy per template, because the two rendered the identical fragment and nothing kept them in step -- a wording or gating change made on the catalogue would leave the homepage quietly rendering the old card. Takes `product`, which both callers already have in scope from their own {% for %}. listing_price_display, not get_display_price: a card standing for a format group shows the range its formats span, since the member the card happens to link to was chosen by format_order and not by the visitor. An ungrouped card gets its own price back unchanged, "Pre-order: " prefix and all. The pay-what-you-want notice is gated on listing_shows_pwyw_notice rather than on is_pwyw, so it appears only where the number above it really is a suggestion for every format the card stands for -- never under a range, most of which is other rows' fixed prices, and never where a same-priced sibling charges that figure outright. The format summary is what stops collapsing the duplicate cards from also taking away the only sign in the catalogue that the other formats exist.
